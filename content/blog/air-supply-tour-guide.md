## Choosing between the cities

If the US dates fit your plans, inspect Hollywood, Rockford and Rancho Mirage before treating a higher-priced date elsewhere as your only option. Within TicketNetwork's snapshots for the dates in this table, Rockford was USD 71.30 and Rancho Mirage was USD 78.20, captured on 2026-11-10 at 09:56 UTC. On Vivid Seats, Hollywood was USD 78.00 and Rancho Mirage was USD 82.00 at 13:05 UTC that day. These examples keep each site separate; they do not establish which seller has the best equivalent seat.

For a Canadian date, start with the city you can attend. Abbotsford, Winnipeg and Ottawa have their own dated figures in the table. A smaller ticket figure in another city does not account for your travel, accommodation or the seat being offered, and this page does not calculate an all-in trip budget.

Once a city looks suitable, open that exact event and check the ticket type, whether the listing covers the quantity you need, and how delivery works. A comparison between cities is only a shortlist; it cannot replace inspecting the listing you intend to buy.

## Check the listing before you commit

These snapshots do not measure a price trend, and a lower figure in one city does not show that waiting will make another date cheaper. If your plans depend on one night, make the decision using that night's actual listing rather than a figure for somewhere else.
