If price matters most, start with Hollywood, Rockford and Rancho Mirage: on 2026-11-09, StubHub International listed USD 63 for Hollywood, TicketNetwork's lowest listed price across its dates was USD 71 for Rockford, and Vivid Seats' lowest typical listing was USD 75 for Hollywood. Nothing can be said yet about movement, because the captured data carries no 30-day history for these dates.

Every figure below is one site's lowest listed price for that date when it was captured on 2026-11-09, in the currency the site listed. It is not a checkout total, and fees, delivery and the final price are set by the ticket site, so confirm them there before paying.

## What has moved

These are figures from a single capture day, and the data holds no 30-day record for any of these dates, so there is no movement to report. None of the listings was flagged as unusually low against the same site's other dates either.

A single day's figure is a starting point, not a promise that the same seat or price will still be there when you check.

## Compare listings for your own date

Once you have a city in mind, open the [Air Supply artist board](/artists/air-supply) and check every site listed for that exact date before you pay, since a figure for one city says nothing about another.
