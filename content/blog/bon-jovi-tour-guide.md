On 2026-11-06, StubHub International's lowest listed price among these dates was USD 80 for East Rutherford, TicketNetwork's was USD 116 for Foxborough, and Vivid Seats' was USD 97 for Toronto. The captured data holds no 30-day movement for any of these dates, so treat this as one day's picture rather than a trend.

Every figure below is a single site's lowest listed price for that date when it was captured on 2026-11-06. It is not a checkout total, and fees, delivery and the final price are set by each ticket site, so confirm them there before you pay.

Reading down each site's column tells you where that site's lowest listing landed.

For StubHub International, East Rutherford came in at USD 80, with Chicago at USD 86 and Foxborough at USD 92. No StubHub International figure was captured for Toronto.

For TicketNetwork, the three cities it was priced in sat close together: USD 116 in Foxborough, USD 117 in East Rutherford and USD 127 in Chicago. There is no TicketNetwork figure for Toronto either.

Vivid Seats is the only site with a figure across all four cities here. Its Toronto listing at USD 97 was its lowest among these dates, followed by USD 100 in East Rutherford, USD 109 in Chicago and USD 112 in Foxborough.

If you are flexible on city, East Rutherford on 2027-07-15 had low figures on both StubHub International and Vivid Seats in this capture. If you are set on Toronto, the only figure in this data is Vivid Seats' listing.

None of these listings is flagged as sitting unusually far below the same site's other dates. A low headline figure can still be a single seat or a restricted view, and the guide on [how to read a ticket listing](/guides/how-to-read-a-ticket-listing) shows what to check before you rely on one.

## Compare listings for your date

Once you have a city in mind, open the [Bon Jovi artist board](/artists/bon-jovi) and compare the listed prices for that exact date before you pay. Prices captured on one day can differ from what a ticket site shows when you visit, so the figures here are a starting point, not a quote.

If you are weighing the original seller against other ticket sites, the guide to [primary vs resale concert tickets](/guides/primary-vs-resale-concert-tickets) sets out the difference.
