**These concert dates have passed.** The guidance below relates to the 26 and 27 September 2026 Falcon Stadium dates listed on Bruno Mars’ official tour page. It should not be used as the access plan for a future concert. [Check current Bruno Mars dates and ticket links](/artists/bruno-mars) for upcoming shows, then read the venue's instructions for the date you choose.

Falcon Stadium is inside the United States Air Force Academy, so the September 2026 guidance covered access to the installation as well as the stadium. A concert ticket identifies the show and admission category; access instructions need to be checked separately for everyone travelling together.

## Start with the instructions for your concert

The Academy’s general visitor page describes sponsored passes, identification checks and screening. It also recognises special-event access arrangements. Those general rules are useful background, but they are not enough to tell every concertgoer which pass, gate or registration process applies on their date.

Read the Academy’s concert information and the messages attached to your booking before you travel. If they do not explain your circumstances, ask the Academy’s Pass and Registration Office to confirm. Do not use a registration link for a different event, or assume that an ordinary visitor pass covers your concert.

Check for each person in your group whether advance registration is required, which original identification they need, and whether their citizenship or documents require an in-person visit. The Academy publishes separate instructions for non-U.S. citizens. Its published office hours have not always agreed between one section of the site and another, so confirm any appointment you need directly with the office rather than planning around a quoted opening time.

## Match the arrival plan to your ticket

The Academy’s floor-seating guidance directs floor ticketholders through the South Gate and into the stadium at Gate 3, with wristband collection inside. It also gives separate instructions for rideshare arrivals. Read that guidance if you hold a floor ticket; do not assume the same route applies to every seating category.

Before leaving, keep the concert date, ticket category, parking or rideshare instructions and entry guidance together. If friends bought different ticket types, compare the instructions before agreeing a meeting point. Leave time for the checks described by the Academy and consult the latest event guidance for arrival times.

The concert has its own published bag policy too. Check its size limits and exceptions before packing, particularly if you need to carry medical items or supplies for an infant. A bag accepted at another venue may not meet this event’s rules.
