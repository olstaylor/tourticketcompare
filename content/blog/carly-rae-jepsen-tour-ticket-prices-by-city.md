## How this was worked out

- **Main table: one site.** Every price in the first table is from Vivid Seats, the site with recent listed prices in the most cities for this tour. The second table is StubHub International, labelled and in date order. Sites are shown side by side but not ranked.
- **One country, one currency.** The tour also has dates in Canada (7). They are left out, because comparing across countries would mix markets and currencies.
- **Next date only.** A city's figure is its first upcoming date, not the lowest of all its dates.
- **Recent prices only.** A price is used only if it was captured in the 72 hours before this page was updated. Captured times are in the table.
- **Coverage.** 17 of the 20 cities on the tour in the United States are priced. 3 cities in the United States had no recent listed price on Vivid Seats and are left out: Chicago, Philadelphia, Salt Lake City.

## What this does and doesn't say

- **These are listed prices.** Starting prices are recorded separately from each ticket site, in its source currency. Seats, ticket quantities and included fees may differ, so these are not like-for-like totals. Check the full order total, delivery and terms on the ticket site.
- **A city gap is not a forecast.** A city that's high today can drop, and a low one can rise. Nothing here says when to buy or why a city is priced as it is.
- **Only the dates TourTicketCompare tracks.** The figures describe those dates on Vivid Seats, not every ticket for the tour.
