Metallica's Life Burns Faster residency at Sphere in Las Vegas plays on Thursdays and Saturdays, and each Thursday and Saturday pair is billed as a No Repeat Weekend: two shows with two different sets, the format the band used on the M72 World Tour. When the dates were extended, the fan club sale offered both single-night tickets and two-night No Repeat Weekend tickets. So a Metallica Sphere listing can differ in a way most listings don't: it may be for one night or for two.

The [Metallica Las Vegas date comparison](/artists/metallica/ticket-prices#priceGuideCity-las-vegas-united-states) lists each Sphere night as its own row, with the ticket sites that have a checked link for that night. ## Thursday, Saturday or both

The two nights of a weekend are not interchangeable. No listing can promise which songs a night will include, only that the two nights of a pair are billed as different sets. If seeing a broad slice of the catalogue matters to you, the pair is the way to get it. If one night is enough, either works, and the choice comes down to the date, the seat and the total.

Thursday and Saturday nights can also price differently on resale, because demand for each date is separate. Compare the two nights of the weekend you want before settling, rather than assuming they cost the same.

## What to check on the listing

- **One night or two.** A listing should say whether it covers a single night or both nights of a weekend. Confirm the admission covered in the ticket details; do not infer it from a single displayed date. The [band’s FAQ](https://www.metallica.com/help/2026-02-25-sphere-faqs.html) distinguishes single-night and two-night tickets, and says a two-night ticket transfers as a unit.
- **The date.** Every listing has the same artist, venue and show name. The date is the only thing that tells them apart, so check it on the listing and on the order summary.
- **The seat.** Where the listing places you matters as much as the night. The [guide to reading a ticket listing](/guides/how-to-read-a-ticket-listing) covers section, row and restriction notes.
- **Delivery.** Resale tickets for a residency night are often transferred close to the date. The [delivery and transfer guide](/guides/ticket-delivery-and-transfer-timing) explains what to expect.
