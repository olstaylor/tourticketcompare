## The spread on each site

Read each column on its own. The two sites are separate listings, and a figure on one says nothing about the other.

On TicketNetwork, the lowest listed price among these dates was USD 82 in Detroit and the highest was USD 124 in Tempe.

On Vivid Seats, the lowest was USD 77 in Detroit and the highest was USD 109 in Edmonton.

So if you can travel, the Michigan dates in Detroit and Grand Rapids sat toward the low end of both columns on the day of the check. If you are tied to one city, the column for that city is the only part of the table that matters to you, and the gap between a low and a high date on one site was a matter of tens of dollars, not hundreds.

## Compare listed prices for your date

Prices on resale sites shift, and these figures are a single day's check. Once you have a city in mind, open the [Dropkick Murphys artist board](/artists/dropkick-murphys) and compare the listings for that exact date before paying.

If a listing comes from a reseller rather than the venue's own seller, [primary vs resale concert tickets](/guides/primary-vs-resale-concert-tickets) explains the difference. For when tickets reach you, see [ticket delivery and transfer timing](/guides/ticket-delivery-and-transfer-timing).
