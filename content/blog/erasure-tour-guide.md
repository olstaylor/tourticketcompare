Read each column on its own. Across the ten dates it priced, Vivid Seats' listings ran from USD 131 in Manchester to USD 325 in Birmingham. Across its six priced dates, StubHub International's ran from USD 133 in Glasgow to USD 309 in Hull. The two sites are separate listings, and a gap between their figures for one date says nothing about which listing suits you.

If a city matters more to you than the figure, the spread within one site is the useful part. Liverpool, Glasgow and Bournemouth all sit near the bottom of the Vivid Seats column, while Birmingham and Hull sit near the top.

No listing in this data was flagged as unusually low against the same site's other dates, so there is no single figure here that needs extra caution beyond the usual check of the listing itself. For what to look for on a resale listing, [how to read a ticket listing](/guides/how-to-read-a-ticket-listing) covers it.

Once you have a city in mind, open the [Erasure artist board](/artists/erasure) and compare the listed prices for that exact date before paying, since a figure for one city tells you nothing about another.
