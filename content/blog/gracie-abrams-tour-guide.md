Over the past 30 days almost every figure moved down, and Nashville on TicketNetwork is the one that went up.

Every figure below is that site's lowest listed price for that date when it was captured on 2026-11-07, in the currency shown. It is not a checkout total, and it is not a statement about what is on sale; confirm the full price on the ticket site before you pay.

## What moved over the last 30 days

Most of these figures are lower than they were a month earlier. Glendale fell on every site: StubHub International went from USD 47 to USD 25, TicketNetwork from USD 69 to USD 38 and Vivid Seats from USD 53 to USD 31. Chicago fell too, with TicketNetwork going from USD 122 to USD 76. Oakland, Inglewood and Seattle all dropped on every site, and Atlanta and Boston moved down too.

A few did not follow. TicketNetwork's Nashville figure rose from USD 62 to USD 71. Vivid Seats in Nashville stayed at USD 70, TicketNetwork in Charlotte stayed at USD 62, and Vivid Seats in Portland was about the same, USD 67 then USD 68. Austin has no figure from 30 days earlier, so there is nothing to compare there.

This is what already happened. It says nothing about where prices go next.

## Listings worth checking first

Some of the lowest figures sit far below that site's other dates: all three Oakland listings (StubHub International USD 21, TicketNetwork USD 22, Vivid Seats USD 25), StubHub International USD 22 and Vivid Seats USD 27 in Inglewood, and StubHub International USD 23 in Seattle. Open the listing on the ticket site and check what it actually is before you plan around it. The guide on [how to read a ticket listing](/guides/how-to-read-a-ticket-listing) covers what to look at, and [ticket delivery and transfer timing](/guides/ticket-delivery-and-transfer-timing) explains when tickets usually reach you.
