## What moved over the last 30 days

This is history, not a forecast. Prices can move either way from here.

Upward moves were common. Boston went from USD 116 to USD 133 on StubHub International, USD 152 to USD 194 on TicketNetwork and USD 131 to USD 153 on Vivid Seats. Chicago rose from USD 168 to USD 182 on StubHub International, USD 246 to USD 268 on TicketNetwork and USD 191 to USD 204 on Vivid Seats. Newark followed the same direction: USD 129 to USD 146 on StubHub International, USD 189 to USD 214 on TicketNetwork, USD 148 to USD 166 on Vivid Seats.

Vivid Seats also rose in Belmont Park (USD 133 to USD 141), Hamilton (USD 165 to USD 186) and Orlando (USD 127 to USD 130). Detroit was split: StubHub International rose from USD 102 to USD 119 and Vivid Seats from USD 117 to USD 136, while TicketNetwork stayed at USD 136.

A few listings went the other way. Cincinnati dropped from USD 93 to USD 82 on StubHub International and from USD 137 to USD 91 on TicketNetwork. Charlotte eased from USD 111 to USD 107 on StubHub International, with TicketNetwork unchanged at USD 144 and Vivid Seats up slightly, from USD 124 to USD 126. Philadelphia moved in both directions: TicketNetwork fell from USD 153 to USD 129, while StubHub International rose from USD 111 to USD 126 and Vivid Seats from USD 124 to USD 147. Quebec on TicketNetwork was about the same, USD 200 then USD 202.

## Two Orlando listings to check first

The Orlando figures on StubHub International (USD 51, down from USD 113) and TicketNetwork (USD 54, down from USD 147) sit far below what those sites list for their other dates. They may be fine. Still, open the listing on the ticket site and check the section, quantity and full price before relying on either number. The guide on [how to read a ticket listing](/guides/how-to-read-a-ticket-listing) covers what to look for.
