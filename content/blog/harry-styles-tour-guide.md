Confirm everything on the official event page before buying, because a listing check is not a guarantee about the show or about tickets.

## What actually differs between them

The useful differences here are the region, the weekday and the season you would be travelling in. Nothing in the listings suggests a reason to rank one city's show above another's, so do not go looking for one.

**Weekday.** Most of the dates in the table fall on a Friday. If you are travelling, that usually means leaving after work or taking the Friday off, then having the weekend to get home. The [Sydney Olympic Park](https://www.ticketmaster.com.au/harry-styles-together-together-sydney-olympic-park-12-12-2026/event/250064346C5043CE) date is a Saturday, which leaves Sunday free for the trip back. [Dublin](https://www.ticketmaster.ie/harry-styles-together-together-dublin-18-08-2027/event/18006529A226B959) and [London](https://www.ticketmaster.co.uk/harry-styles-together-together-london-25-08-2027/event/2300650EC5AF20F8) are both Wednesdays. A midweek stadium show is easy if you live nearby and harder if you need a hotel and time off on either side.

**Region and timing.** The Australian stops come before the North American ones, and the European stops come after. If one region is a long way from you, the gap before its date gives you more room to plan travel and leave.

**Which country site lists it.** Ticketmaster lists each city on its own country site: .com.au for the Australian stops, .ca for Toronto, .ie for Dublin, .co.uk for London and .com for the US cities.

## Make sure two listings are the same show

Resale listings and screenshots do not always describe a show the way Ticketmaster does. Before comparing two prices, check that both match on all of these:

- **Date in venue-local time.** The dates above are local to each venue. A site showing times in your own zone can make a date look different.
- **Venue name.** Marvel Stadium, Accor Stadium, Rose Bowl and so on, not just the city.
- **City as listed.** Ticketmaster lists the Australian venues under Docklands and Sydney Olympic Park. Another site may use a broader place name, so match the venue as well.
- **Ticket type and quantity.** Same section type, same number of tickets.
