Listed resale prices for 2027 shows mostly drifted down after they first appeared, but it depended on the tour. TourTicketCompare keeps a daily record of the lowest listed price on Vivid Seats, TicketNetwork and StubHub International. For 1,039 shows in 2027 by 68 artists, it compared each site's average lowest price in its first seven recorded days with 18 to 24 November 2026. That made 2,191 comparisons. The price was lower at 1,179 (54%), higher at 715 (33%) and within 2% at 297. The typical change was a fall of 3.2%.

First recorded days run from 22 July to 3 October, so the gap between the two readings is about 7 to 17 weeks. This is a record of what happened on these tours, not a forecast.

Each row compares one site only with its own earlier listings. It does not say which site is lower for any show.

Falls outnumbered rises on all three sites. On Vivid Seats the margin was slim. Across artists, 45 of the 68 had more falls than rises.

## What this does and doesn't say

- **It is the lowest listed price, not every seat.** One cheap listing can set it, and a different listing becoming the cheapest can move it.
- **It is not a checkout total.** Fees and delivery differ between sites and listings. Check the full total before you pay.
- **Prices are in US dollars as listed.** Figures are compared with themselves, so currency and fees cancel, but levels are not compared between countries.
- **It is not a rule.** Prices rose at a third of comparisons, and a tour that fell in autumn may not keep falling.
- **Sample:** 1,039 shows listed by 3 October; shows added later are not included.
