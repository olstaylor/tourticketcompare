TourTicketCompare records each resale site's lowest listed price for the upcoming dates it tracks, and keeps a daily record. This page compares, for each date and each site, the price at the start of the period with the price at the end, and counts which way it went. The period runs between 16 September and 17 October 2026.

| Site | Dates | Lower | Higher | About the same |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vivid Seats | 173 | 108 | 40 | 25 |
| TicketNetwork | 402 | 209 | 151 | 42 |
| StubHub International | 252 | 120 | 107 | 25 |

"About the same" means within 2% either way. Each count compares one date with itself on one site. Sites list different seats with different fees, so this page does not compare one site with another, and a count says nothing about which site is lower for a given show.

## What this does and doesn't say

- **It is a record of the last 30 days, not a forecast.** A date that went down this month can go up next month. Whether prices tend to fall as a show gets closer is a different question, and TourTicketCompare doesn't have enough history yet to answer it reliably.
- **A listed price is not a checkout total.** Fees, delivery and seat location differ between listings and between sites.
- **No price figures here.** For the current price of any date, with the time it was captured, open the artist's page.

To buy for one of these shows, start on the artist's page, which links the official sale and any checked resale listings for each date.
