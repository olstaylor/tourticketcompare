## Compare listed prices for your date

Pick the city and date that suit you first. Then open the [Kings Of Leon artist board](/artists/kings-of-leon) and compare listings for that exact date before you pay. A figure for one date tells you nothing reliable about another.

When you reach a ticket site, check that the listing names the same venue and date as the event page linked above.
