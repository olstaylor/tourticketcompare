## Weekday matters more than it looks

The Saturday dates are Vancouver, San Antonio, Kansas City, Indianapolis and Salt Lake City. If you are travelling, a Saturday show lets you arrive Friday or Saturday morning and head home Sunday without losing a workday.

El Paso, Orchard Park and Cleveland fall on Tuesdays, and Fayetteville on a Wednesday. Those suit people who live nearby, or anyone happy to take a day or two off. Las Vegas and San Diego are Thursdays.

If you live between regions, line up the dates. Kansas City on a Saturday and Fayetteville on the Wednesday before it are close together in the calendar. So are Indianapolis and Orchard Park. The table gives you the dates. How far you will drive or fly is yours to work out.

## Before you pay

Every listing shares the same artist name. What tells them apart is the city, venue and date, so check them against each other.

- **The date and city.** Confirm both on the listing and again on the order summary. A mistake here is easy to make and hard to undo.
- **The seat.** Section, row, whether the seats are together, and any restriction notes. The [guide to reading a ticket listing](/guides/how-to-read-a-ticket-listing) covers each label.
- **Who is selling.** The official Ticketmaster page and a resale site are different kinds of seller. The [primary versus resale guide](/guides/primary-vs-resale-concert-tickets) explains what changes.
- **When it arrives.** Resale tickets are often transferred close to the show. The [delivery and transfer guide](/guides/ticket-delivery-and-transfer-timing) explains the usual timing.
- **The total.** Compare the final checkout total for the same seats and quantity, not the first figure shown.
