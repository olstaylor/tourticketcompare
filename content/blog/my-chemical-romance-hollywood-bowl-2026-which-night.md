Choose the billing you want before comparing tickets. My Chemical Romance's Hollywood Bowl dates on 21, 23 and 24 October carry **The Black Parade 2026** title. The 30 October listing names **The Used** as special guest; 31 October names **Thrice**. Those final two listings also start an hour earlier. The same artist and venue do not make these five purchases interchangeable.

The [My Chemical Romance date board](/artists/my-chemical-romance) is the starting point for comparing ticket options for the exact night you choose. A figure on a different night's card is not a quote for your preferred show.

## What the different billing lets you decide

If seeing The Used is part of the reason for going, shortlist 30 October. If it is Thrice, shortlist 31 October. If the advertised Black Parade tour is the attraction, start with 21, 23 and 24 October and read the specific event details before paying.

That is a useful distinction, but it has a boundary: **an event title is not a setlist**. The later listings do not establish which MCR songs will be played, and the Halloween date alone does not establish a special performance. Nothing in this comparison promises a particular song or an identical show across nights. Guest billing can change; recheck the promoter's event page when booking.

There is a practical difference even if the guest does not matter to you. A 7pm start leaves an hour less for getting from work or a flight to the venue than an 8pm start. Check the event's arrival instructions separately. Do not treat either scheduled start as the time MCR will take the stage.

## Compare tickets within your shortlist

Once the night is fixed, compare the same quantity and the same kind of seat. A pair together, two separate seats and a single ticket answer different needs, even when their first displayed figures look similar. Check section, row, seat arrangement and any view restriction on the provider's listing. The [ticket-listing guide](/guides/how-to-read-a-ticket-listing) explains those fields.

The [MCR ticket-price page](/artists/my-chemical-romance/ticket-prices) organises tracked dates and any eligible provider-listed snapshots by city. Use each date's own comparison links. Any displayed figure belongs to that event and provider at the capture time shown; it is not a final checkout total or evidence that a particular seat remains purchasable.

If several nights suit you, open each separately and compare actual listings that meet your needs, including the final order total. There is no supported claim here that a Wednesday, a Halloween show or a later-added date costs less. The [comparison guide](/guides/how-to-compare-concert-ticket-prices) covers how to check totals without mixing unlike tickets.

## Keep the date attached to the purchase

Before paying, match the full date on the listing and order summary to the night you selected. Then check the ticket delivery deadline against your travel plans. A confirmation email and a transferred ticket are different things; the [delivery and transfer guide](/guides/ticket-delivery-and-transfer-timing) explains what to confirm with a resale seller.

The useful order is simple: billing, date, seats, total, delivery. Comparing figures first can leave you with a ticket for a concert you did not mean to choose.
