Every figure below is one site's lowest listed price for that date when it was captured on 2026-11-07, in that site's own currency. It is not a checkout total: fees, delivery and the final price are shown only on the ticket site itself. Each site is a separate listing, so read the columns side by side rather than against each other.

## What moved over the last 30 days

This is history, a comparison with each listing's figure thirty days before capture, and it says nothing about where prices go next.

Detroit fell on all three sites. StubHub International went from USD 81 to USD 58, TicketNetwork from USD 120 to USD 85, and Vivid Seats from USD 93 to USD 63.

Baltimore fell too: TicketNetwork from USD 99 to USD 63 and Vivid Seats from USD 93 to USD 63. TicketNetwork's Montreal listing went from USD 114 to USD 86.

Columbus split. Vivid Seats went from USD 75 to USD 61, while TicketNetwork went from USD 70 to USD 77.

Orlando moved the most in the other direction. StubHub International went from USD 20 to USD 62 and TicketNetwork from USD 30 to USD 87, while Vivid Seats went from USD 90 to USD 66.

Boston edged up on every site: StubHub International from USD 78 to USD 81, TicketNetwork from USD 91 to USD 94, Vivid Seats from USD 88 to USD 91. Vivid Seats in Brooklyn and Toronto and TicketNetwork in Chicago stayed about the same.
