## Compare your own date before paying

Once you have a city in mind, open the [Nickelback price guide](/artists/nickelback/ticket-prices) and the [Nickelback artist board](/artists/nickelback) for that exact date, and compare listed prices across the sites before you commit. A listed price can change between capture and checkout, so confirm the figure, the section and the final total on the ticket site itself.
