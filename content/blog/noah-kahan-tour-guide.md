Vivid Seats' lowest was for [Protective Stadium in Birmingham on 2027-04-27](https://www.ticketmaster.com/noah-kahan-the-last-of-the-birmingham-alabama-04-27-2027/event/2000655CAB442FB5) at USD 398. The site has no 30-day price history for these dates yet, so it cannot show how they have moved.

Every figure here is one ticket site's lowest listed price for one date when it was checked on 2026-11-07. It is not a checkout total, and it says nothing about what is left to buy. Confirm fees, delivery and the final price on the ticket site before you pay.
