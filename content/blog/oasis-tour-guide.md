Across the Oasis dates in this snapshot, each ticket site's lowest listed price was for [Las Vegas on 2027-08-20 at Allegiant Stadium](https://www.ticketmaster.com/oasis-live-27-las-vegas-nevada-08-20-2027/event/170065119F2C9700), when checked on 2026-11-02: StubHub International listed USD 173, TicketNetwork USD 192 and Vivid Seats USD 194. Over the previous 30 days most listed prices went up, while TicketNetwork's lowest listing fell for both Glasgow and Las Vegas.

Those figures are each site's lowest listed price for that one date when it was checked. They are not checkout totals, and they say nothing about what is still on sale. Fees, delivery and the final total show up only on the ticket site itself.

## What moved over the last 30 days

This is history only. It shows where each listing was, not where it is heading.

- **Glasgow:** StubHub International went from USD 252 to USD 309. TicketNetwork came down from USD 407 to USD 323. Vivid Seats was about the same, USD 430 then USD 424.
- **Manchester:** all three rose. StubHub International from USD 318 to USD 329, TicketNetwork from USD 316 to USD 335, Vivid Seats from USD 385 to USD 440.
- **Foxborough:** all three rose. StubHub International from USD 242 to USD 271, TicketNetwork from USD 367 to USD 400, Vivid Seats from USD 271 to USD 307.
- **Las Vegas:** StubHub International rose from USD 150 to USD 173. TicketNetwork dropped from USD 255 to USD 192. Vivid Seats was about the same, USD 191 then USD 194.
- **Stevenage:** StubHub International rose from USD 380 to USD 400.

A lowest listing can belong to a single seat in a poor spot, so a rise or fall in one figure does not mean the whole date moved with it.

## Unusually low listings

None of the figures captured here was flagged as sitting far below that site's other dates. Even so, open the listing before you trust any lowest price. Check the section, whether it is one seat or a pair, and how the ticket will reach you. The [guide to reading a ticket listing](/guides/how-to-read-a-ticket-listing) covers what to look for, and the [delivery and transfer timing guide](/guides/ticket-delivery-and-transfer-timing) explains why some resale tickets arrive late. If you are unsure whether you are buying from the original seller or a resale marketplace, the [primary vs resale guide](/guides/primary-vs-resale-concert-tickets) sets out the difference.

## Compare your own date

Once you have picked a city, compare listed prices for that exact date rather than relying on the table above. Start with the [Oasis artist board](/artists/oasis) and the [Oasis price guide](/artists/oasis/ticket-prices), then confirm the total, fees and delivery on the ticket site before you pay.
