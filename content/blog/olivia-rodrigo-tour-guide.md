Every figure here is one ticket site's lowest listed price for one date when it was checked on 2026-11-02, in that site's own currency. It is not a live price and not a final total. Fees, delivery and the checkout total are set by each ticket site, so confirm them there before you pay.

## What moved over the last 30 days

These are past movements in each site's listing, not a forecast. A listing that fell last month can rise next month.

The two northern California dates moved down on every site. For [Oakland on 2026-12-11](https://www.ticketmaster.com/olivia-rodrigo-the-unraveled-tour-oakland-california-12-11-2026/event/1C00649DD84D193A), StubHub International went from USD 353 to USD 324, TicketNetwork from USD 526 to USD 474, and Vivid Seats from USD 397 to USD 356. ## Before you rely on a listing

None of these listings was marked as sitting unusually far below the same site's other dates. That does not make a low figure safe to trust as it stands. A lowest listed price can be a single seat, a restricted view or a ticket with late delivery.

Open the listing itself and check the section, row, quantity and delivery method before paying. The guide on [how to read a ticket listing](/guides/how-to-read-a-ticket-listing) covers what to look for. [Ticket delivery and transfer timing](/guides/ticket-delivery-and-transfer-timing) explains why a resale ticket may not reach you straight away. If you are weighing a resale site against the original seller, see [primary vs resale concert tickets](/guides/primary-vs-resale-concert-tickets).
