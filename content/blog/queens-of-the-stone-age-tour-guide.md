## Picking your date

Start with the city you can actually reach, then compare that one date across sites. A lower listed price in another city stops looking low once travel is added.

Most of these figures come from resale sites, so read [primary vs resale concert tickets](/guides/primary-vs-resale-concert-tickets) if you are unsure what you are buying. For the December dates in particular, check [ticket delivery and transfer timing](/guides/ticket-delivery-and-transfer-timing) so the tickets reach you before the show.

When you have a city in mind, go to the [Queens Of The Stone Age artist board](/artists/queens-of-the-stone-age) and compare the listed prices for your exact date before you pay.
