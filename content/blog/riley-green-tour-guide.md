## What moved over 30 days

This compares each listing with the same site's figure 30 days before the check. It is history only and says nothing about where prices go next.

Edmonton and Calgary moved down on both sites. In Calgary, Vivid Seats went from USD 161 to USD 116 and TicketNetwork from USD 133 to USD 120. In Edmonton, TicketNetwork went from USD 117 to USD 102 and Vivid Seats from USD 113 to USD 106.

Eugene and Greenville moved up on both sites. Eugene went from USD 82 to USD 110 on TicketNetwork and from USD 85 to USD 124 on Vivid Seats. Greenville went from USD 94 to USD 128 on TicketNetwork and from USD 97 to USD 138 on Vivid Seats.

Tulsa's Vivid Seats listing dropped from USD 81 to USD 67, while TicketNetwork held at USD 82, as it also did in Seattle and Wichita. Seattle rose on the other two sites: StubHub International went from USD 73 to USD 88 and Vivid Seats from USD 86 to USD 96.

The rest moved in mixed directions. Fort Worth fell on StubHub International (USD 89 to USD 79) and TicketNetwork (USD 131 to USD 107) but rose slightly on Vivid Seats (USD 92 to USD 95). Saskatoon fell on Vivid Seats (USD 113 to USD 97) and rose on TicketNetwork (USD 101 to USD 114). Raleigh fell on TicketNetwork (USD 107 to USD 86) and rose on Vivid Seats (USD 85 to USD 94), with no earlier StubHub International figure to compare. Knoxville's TicketNetwork listing fell from USD 110 to USD 98 while its Vivid Seats listing went from USD 85 to USD 89. Rogers and Wichita moved by USD 5 or less on both sites.
