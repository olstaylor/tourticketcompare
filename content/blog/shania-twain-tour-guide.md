StubHub International had listed prices for four of these dates. Its lowest was USD 174 in Exeter and its highest was USD 330 in Woodstock.

Vivid Seats had listed prices for six. Its lowest was USD 224 in Cardiff and its highest was USD 333 in Exeter.

Exeter sits at the low end of one site's figures and the high end of the other's. That is the clearest reason to look at every listing for your date rather than one: each site is a separate marketplace with its own listings, and its figures say nothing about the other's.

There is no 30-day history behind these numbers, so nothing here shows whether any date has been rising or falling.

Dublin has no captured price on either site, though the date itself is confirmed on its [Marlay Park listing](https://www.ticketmaster.ie/shania-twain-the-places-ill-go-dublin-26-06-2027/event/18006556AA68D625).

This table is not the full list. It holds one date per city, and the [Shania Twain artist board](/artists/shania-twain) lists other tracked dates, in these cities and in others.
