Choose a Trans-Siberian Orchestra matinee if an earlier start fits your group and journey home; choose the evening if the afternoon clashes with work or travel. **Neither session is established as the less expensive option by the evidence here.** For the 2026 winter tour, several cities have two performances on the same date, so the start time is part of identifying the ticket you need.

Start with the [Trans-Siberian Orchestra date board](/artists/trans-siberian-orchestra), then check the full date and time on the provider's listing. Matching the city and day is not enough when the venue hosts both sessions.

The following schedules were checked against official artist or venue pages on 11 October 2026. All times are local to the venue. This is a selection of confirmed examples, not the complete tour schedule.

TSO's [Denver page](https://www.trans-siberian.com/pages/market/denver--co?event=Denver_CO) and [Philadelphia page](https://www.trans-siberian.com/pages/market/philadelphia--pa?event=Philadelphia_PA) list both sessions. [Nationwide Arena's Columbus listing](https://www.nationwidearena.com/events/detail/trans-siberian-orchestra-6) does the same.

TTC currently tracks the afternoon performance in each of these three examples. An evening session missing from the date board does not mean the performance does not exist. Use the official schedule to confirm the session you want; the site's checked comparison links apply only to the performance named on their card.

## An afternoon ticket is still a ticket to the advertised production

The official pages describe the 2026 tour as a new staging of **The Ghosts of Christmas Eve**, followed by a second set. They do not label the afternoon performances as shortened children's shows. Equally, that description does not promise identical running times or song selections across sessions.

For a family booking, an earlier start may make the journey home easier to plan. It does not make the production quieter: the event descriptions mention lasers, pyrotechnics and other effects. Check the venue's child admission, hearing protection and accessibility guidance before choosing seats. Do not assume a child enters free or can share an adult's seat.

For an evening booking, work backwards from the scheduled start to the time you need to arrive. A 7.30pm listing is not a confirmed finishing time. If the last train or bus determines whether the evening works, ask the venue about expected timings before committing.

## Compare one performance at a time

The easy mistake is opening two listings for the same date and assuming they describe the same event. Check **city, venue, date and start time** before comparing section, row and ticket quantity. A 3pm ticket should not be treated as admission to the 7.30pm performance unless the actual ticket terms explicitly say so.

For sessions TTC tracks, the [TSO ticket-price page](/artists/trans-siberian-orchestra/ticket-prices) brings together the date-specific comparison links and any eligible listed-price snapshots. A displayed figure is one provider's snapshot for that performance, with its capture time. It does not describe the evening session, a family bundle or the final order total.

A group needs a listing that meets its seating requirements, rather than a headline figure for a single seat. Confirm whether the seats are together and compare the total for everyone attending. The [listing guide](/guides/how-to-read-a-ticket-listing) and [ticket-price comparison guide](/guides/how-to-compare-concert-ticket-prices) cover those checks.

## Delivery matters when someone else is taking the group

If you are buying as a gift or for relatives, establish who will hold the tickets on show day. Nationwide Arena says its mobile tickets are not valid as paper printouts or screenshots; its event page explains the arrangements for guests without a smartphone. Those are Columbus instructions, not a rule to assume for every tour stop.

Check the chosen venue's own entry method and the seller's delivery deadline. The [delivery and transfer guide](/guides/ticket-delivery-and-transfer-timing) helps distinguish an order confirmation from a ticket ready for entry. Choosing the right session is only useful if the person going can receive and use the tickets.
