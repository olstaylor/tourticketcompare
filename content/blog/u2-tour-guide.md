Every figure is that site's lowest listed price for that date when captured on 2026-11-07, with no 30-day history attached, so nothing can be said yet about which way any of them has moved.

If price is your main filter, start with the dates your preferred site lists in the table. Then compare every site for that exact date before you pay.

## Each site's figures in this set

These are the figures captured on 2026-11-07 for the six dates above only, grouped by site. They are not rankings of sites and do not cover other U2 dates.

StubHub International: USD 110 for Stockholm, USD 116 for Ciudad de México, USD 164 for London and USD 171 for Amsterdam.

TicketNetwork: USD 189 for Ciudad de México, USD 215 for Stockholm, USD 294 for Amsterdam and USD 627 for Sevilla.

Vivid Seats: USD 232 for London, and USD 254 each for Barcelona and Sevilla.

Not every site lists every date, so a missing figure only means none was captured for that date. If you are looking at Sevilla, the two figures captured there are Vivid Seats USD 254 and TicketNetwork USD 627; check each site's own listing for that date before deciding.

## Compare your own date

Once you have a city in mind, open the [U2 artist board](/artists/u2) and compare listings for that exact date, including any date not shown in the table above.
