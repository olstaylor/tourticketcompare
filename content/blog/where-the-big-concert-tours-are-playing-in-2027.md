TourTicketCompare tracks the upcoming dates of 101 major touring artists, and as of 17 October 2026 2,074 of those dates fall in 2027. They are spread over 332 cities and 819 venues in 21 countries. Las Vegas has the most, with 72 dates from 18 artists, followed by London (68) and Toronto (61).

These are counts of announced shows on the tours the site follows, not of every concert in 2027. Each one is a date with a checked Ticketmaster event behind it, and each is still listed on its artist's page on this site.

## How these numbers are counted

- **What counts:** an announced 2027 show by an artist whose page is live on TourTicketCompare, matched to an exact Ticketmaster event and not cancelled or postponed. The date is the venue's local date.
- **What doesn't:** shows by artists the site doesn't track, and dates that have already happened.
- **Freshness:** event records are rechecked automatically. The newest recheck in this data set is 16 October 2026.
- **Prices:** this page has no prices. Ticket prices change constantly and are shown, with the time they were captured, on each artist's page.
