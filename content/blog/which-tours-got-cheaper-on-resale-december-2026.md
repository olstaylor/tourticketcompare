TourTicketCompare keeps a daily record of the lowest listed resale price on Vivid Seats, TicketNetwork and StubHub International for the concerts it tracks. For this report it took every upcoming US date that had a price on the same site in both 18 to 20 October and 17 to 19 November 2026, and compared each one with itself a month earlier. That gave 3,220 comparisons across 1,530 dates by 93 artists. The lowest listed price was lower at 1,246 of them, higher at 1,162, and within 2% at the other 812. The typical move was 0%.

That is a change from [last month's report](/blog/which-tours-got-cheaper-on-resale-november-2026), when falls outnumbered rises by about two to one. This month the market was roughly level, and Vivid Seats went the other way.

## Site by site

Each row compares one site only with its own listings a month earlier. Sites list different seats with different fees, so this does not say which site is lower for any show.

| Site | Dates | Lower | Higher | Within 2% | Typical move |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vivid Seats | 1,258 | 380 | 618 | 260 | +1.9% |
| TicketNetwork | 1,417 | 632 | 337 | 448 | -1.1% |
| StubHub International | 545 | 234 | 207 | 104 | -0.3% |

On Vivid Seats, rises outnumbered falls by about three to two. On TicketNetwork, falls outnumbered rises by about two to one. StubHub International was close to even. Last month all three sites showed more falls than rises.

The biggest move was Zach Bryan's 2027 US tour, whose dates run from April to August 2027. Every one of its 22 comparisons was lower. On TicketNetwork, the median lowest listed price across its 11 dates was $198 in mid-October and $95 in mid-November, and on Vivid Seats across 11 dates $165 and $97 respectively (USD, listed prices, medians of 18 to 20 October and 17 to 19 November 2026).

Gracie Abrams dates, which run from December 2026 to March 2027, fell for a second month running: 75 of 99 comparisons were lower. On TicketNetwork the median across 33 dates was $62 in mid-October and $52 in mid-November.

## Tours that did not make the list

- **Greta Van Fleet**, last month's biggest faller at -43%, was flat this month: across 35 dates the typical move was 0%, with a median lowest listed price of $71 at both ends.
- **Nickelback**'s 30 dates moved in different directions on different sites: down 6.2% on TicketNetwork, up 7.8% on Vivid Seats and up 10.6% on StubHub International.
- **Metallica**'s 23 dates had a typical move of -2.9%, below this report's 5% bar.

## What this does and doesn't say

- **It is a record of one month, not a forecast.** A tour that fell this month can rise next month. Every date was a month closer to the show at the second reading, which is part of what this measures.
- **It is the lowest listed price, not every seat.** A big move can be one cheap listing appearing or selling.
- **It is US dates only,** in US dollars, and only dates still more than a week away on 20 November 2026. Cities that share a name with a non-US city were left out.
- **A listed price is not a checkout total.** Fees, delivery and seat location differ between listings and between sites.
- **The readings end on 19 November.** The comparison was made on 20 November 2026, so prices have moved since.

Each reading is the median of the last daily check on each of three days. Dates without a price on at least two of the three days at both ends were left out, and so were comparisons where either reading was $1,000 or more, which are treated as placeholders. TourTicketCompare repeats this comparison each month.

For the current listed price of any date, with the time it was captured, open the artist's page. The [guide to why ticket prices change](/guides/why-ticket-prices-change) explains what moves them.
