TourTicketCompare keeps a daily record of the lowest listed resale price on Vivid Seats, TicketNetwork and StubHub International for the concerts it tracks. For this report it took every upcoming US date that had a price on the same site in both 5 to 7 October and 3 to 5 November 2026, and compared each one with itself a month earlier. That gave 2,344 comparisons across 1,072 dates by 71 artists. The lowest listed price was lower at 1,201 of them, higher at 621, and within 2% at the rest. The typical move was a fall of about 2%.

## Site by site

Each row compares one site only with its own listings a month earlier. Sites list different seats with different fees, so this does not say which site is lower for any show.

| Site | Dates | Artists | Lower | Higher | Within 2% | Typical move |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vivid Seats | 965 | 68 | 475 | 251 | 239 | -1.7% |
| TicketNetwork | 981 | 71 | 526 | 233 | 222 | -3.1% |
| StubHub International | 398 | 49 | 200 | 137 | 61 | -2.3% |

Falls outnumbered rises on every site.

"Lower" and "Higher" count site-and-date comparisons, so a date listed on three sites counts up to three times.

The biggest move was Greta Van Fleet's 2027 tour. Every one of its 69 comparisons was lower. On Vivid Seats, the median lowest listed price across its 35 dates was $123 in early October and $65 in early November, and on TicketNetwork across 34 dates $140 and $86 respectively (USD, listed prices, medians of 5 to 7 October and 3 to 5 November 2026).

Gracie Abrams dates, which run from December 2026 to March 2027, were lower at 86 of 99 comparisons. On Vivid Seats the median across 33 dates was $63 in early October and $46 in early November.

## Big tours that barely moved

Some of the most-searched tours had no clear direction over the month. Olivia Rodrigo's 35 US dates had a typical move of 0% across 105 comparisons. Metallica's 18 dates moved in different directions on different sites. Hans Zimmer's 26 dates rose on every site, but the typical move (+4.6%) was just under this report's 5% bar.

## What this does and doesn't say

- **It is a record of one month, not a forecast.** A tour that fell this month can rise next month. Every date was a month closer to the show at the second reading, which is part of what this measures.
- **It is the lowest listed price, not every seat.** A big move can be one cheap listing appearing or selling.
- **It is US dates only,** in US dollars, and only dates still more than a week away on 6 November 2026.
- **A listed price is not a checkout total.** Fees, delivery and seat location differ between listings and between sites.

Each reading is the median of the last daily check on each of three days. Dates without a price on at least two of the three days at both ends were left out (80 comparisons), and so were 5 comparisons with a price of $1,000 or more, which are treated as placeholders. TourTicketCompare will repeat this comparison each month.

For the current listed price of any date, with the time it was captured, open the artist's page. The [guide to why ticket prices change](/guides/why-ticket-prices-change) explains what moves them.
