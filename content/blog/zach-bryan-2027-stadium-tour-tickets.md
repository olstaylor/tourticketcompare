Zach Bryan's 2027 stadium tour went on general sale on Friday 23 October 2026, at 10:00 local time for each venue, after artist and local presales on 21 and 22 October. The tour, as announced, runs from late April to August across North America, then to Australia in December. For most people still looking now, the question is no longer how to get through the queue. It is how to compare what is left.

## Compare the date you want

The [Zach Bryan page](/artists/zach-bryan) lists each checked date with its own ticket sites, and links the Zach Bryan price guide for a date-by-date view. Where a price appears, it is one site's listed price for that one date, with the time it was checked. It isn't the face value or a final checkout total.

## Who opens which leg

Per the same announcement, John Fogerty and Fey Fili open the spring dates, Ryan Bingham and The Texas Gentlemen with Gabriella Rose open the summer dates from Las Vegas to Boise, and Vance Joy and Emma Ogier are named for Australia. If the support act matters to you, check the date before you buy, because it changes by leg, not by city.

## What to check on a stadium resale listing

A stadium listing has more ways to differ than an arena one. Three are worth checking every time.

- **Floor or seats.** A floor ticket may be standing general admission, and the listing should say so. The [floor or seats guide](/blog/floor-or-seats-at-a-stadium-concert) covers the trade-off.
- **Ticket type.** Ticketmaster lists a separate General Admission Early Entry sale for the Seattle date. A listing for that date should say whether early entry is included, and if it doesn't, assume it isn't.
- **Delivery date.** Resale sellers often can't transfer stadium tickets until close to the show. The [delivery and transfer guide](/guides/ticket-delivery-and-transfer-timing) explains what a late delivery date means and when to worry.

The [guide to reading a ticket listing](/guides/how-to-read-a-ticket-listing) goes through the rest. Whatever you buy, check the date and city on the order summary before paying. With two nights each at MetLife, BC Place and Commonwealth Stadium, the date is the field that separates one listing from another.
