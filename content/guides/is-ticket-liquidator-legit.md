**Short answer:** yes, Ticket Liquidator is a legitimate resale marketplace that has been running since 2003. Despite the name, it is not a clearance outlet: it lists tickets from third-party sellers, usually above face value, and backs orders with a 100% Money-Back Guarantee that the tickets arrive before the event and get you in. It is part of the same South Windsor, Connecticut business as TicketNetwork, so expect many of the same listings on both. A postponed show is not refunded.

## Who runs Ticket Liquidator

Ticket Liquidator's company page says the name is a registered trademark of Ticket Software, LLC, and gives the same address as TicketNetwork: 75 Gerber Road East, South Windsor, Connecticut. TicketNetwork describes itself as running "a vast network of websites" where buyers and sellers exchange tickets, and Ticket Liquidator is one of those sites.

In practice that means two things. The seller behind a listing is usually a ticket broker, not Ticket Liquidator itself. And the same seats can appear on Ticket Liquidator and TicketNetwork at once, so compare the two on the final total rather than assuming they are separate stock.

## Why the name is misleading

"Liquidator" suggests leftover tickets sold cheaply. Ticket Liquidator's own FAQ says the opposite: tickets on the site "will therefore usually be priced greater than face value", because sellers paid for them and set their own prices. Its company page repeats that resale prices may be above face value.

Prices can still fall below face value for a show with weak demand, but nothing about the site makes that more likely than on any other resale marketplace.

## What the guarantee covers

Ticket Liquidator's 100% Money-Back Guarantee, as its company page describes it, covers:

- Tickets that are legitimate and valid for entry.
- Delivery before the event.
- Seats comparable to the ones you ordered.
- A refund if the event is cancelled.

It does not cover changing your mind. The FAQ is direct about this: all sales are final, which it says is standard across both primary and resale ticketing.

## Cancelled and postponed shows

- **Cancelled:** the FAQ says a cancelled event is refunded in full, minus shipping. Its COVID-19 FAQ adds a choice between that refund and a credit voucher worth 110% of the original order, less delivery charges, valid for 365 days from issue.
- **Postponed:** tickets stay valid for the new date, with no refund.

If a moved date does not work for you, plan to resell rather than to claim a refund. [What to do if a concert is postponed or cancelled](/guides/what-to-do-if-a-concert-is-postponed-or-cancelled) walks through the options.

## Fees and delivery

The FAQ says a service fee is part of every order and is a percentage of the ticket price. At the time of checking it also listed separate flat charges for email delivery, will-call and local pickup. These can change, so read the order summary before you pay.

Delivery depends on the seller. Some tickets are not printed or issued until a few weeks before the show, and the FAQ says sellers ship as soon as they receive them. A gap between buying and receiving is normal; the guarantee is what covers a ticket that never comes. Note the delivery method on the listing, because a mobile transfer, an email PDF and a will-call pickup each work differently on the day. See [ticket delivery and transfer timing](/guides/ticket-delivery-and-transfer-timing).

## How to check a Ticket Liquidator listing

- Match the artist, date, venue and city exactly. Resale sites list many similar events.
- Read the listing notes for restrictions such as age limits, obstructed views or a delivery date.
- Take the order to the final review screen and compare that total with the same section on the primary seller and on TicketNetwork.
- Pay by card, keep the confirmation email, and save the support details: (860) 768-9263 or customersupport@ticketliquidator.com, per its FAQ.

On TourTicketCompare, a Ticket Liquidator button appears on a date only after its link has been matched to that exact event. No Ticket Liquidator price is shown on the site; open the listing to see one.

## FAQ

**Is Ticket Liquidator a real company?**

Yes. It has operated since 2003 from South Windsor, Connecticut, and is part of the same business as TicketNetwork.

**Is Ticket Liquidator cheaper than other sites?**

Not by design. Its own FAQ says prices are usually above face value, because sellers set them. Compare the checkout total for the same seats on other sites.

**Does Ticket Liquidator refund postponed events?**

No. Its FAQ says tickets for a postponed event are valid for the new date and are not refunded. A cancelled event is refunded, minus shipping.

**Who do I contact about a Ticket Liquidator order?**

Its FAQ lists (860) 768-9263 and customersupport@ticketliquidator.com. Have your order number ready.

**Are Ticket Liquidator and TicketNetwork the same?**

They are separate websites run within the same business, and many listings appear on both. See [is TicketNetwork legit](/guides/is-ticketnetwork-legit).
