Most Vivid Seats promo codes take a fixed amount off once the tickets in your order pass a minimum spend, and that minimum is counted before service and delivery charges. The discount comes off the ticket cost only, never the fees. Vivid Seats shows all-in prices, so the number on an event page already includes its service and delivery charges, and the honest test of any code is the total on the final checkout screen. TourTicketCompare does not publish codes; this guide explains how they behave so you can tell quickly whether one is worth your time.

## How Vivid Seats promo codes work

Vivid Seats' help page on promotional codes sets out the rules that trip most people up:

- **A minimum spend.** Most promotions need the tickets to reach a set amount before they apply. Its example is a fixed-amount code that only applies once the tickets alone, before service and shipping charges, reach the stated purchase total.
- **Ticket cost only.** A code worth a fixed amount reduces the ticket cost, not the total. Service and delivery charges are unchanged.
- **Email offers with no code.** Some promotions arrive as an email with a banner rather than a code. Vivid Seats says to click the banner, choose tickets in the window that opens and check out there; the discount is applied automatically.

To enter a code, choose your tickets, sign in or create an account, and start checkout. In the payment section, open "Apply a Gift Card or Promo Code", enter it, select Apply, and check the updated amount before you pay.

## Why a Vivid Seats code does nothing

When a code is accepted but the total barely moves, or it is rejected, the cause is usually one of these:

- The tickets come in under the minimum. The minimum ignores fees, so an order whose total clears the threshold only because of fees does not qualify.
- The offer was tied to an email link and is being entered as a code on a fresh page.
- The code has expired, or it was meant for a different account or a first order.
- The code came from a coupon site and was never valid for your order.

If a code fails, decide on the full total. Searching for another code while the seats sit in your basket is how people end up paying more for a worse listing.

## What the Vivid Seats price includes

Vivid Seats uses all-in pricing. Its fees article says a service charge and a delivery charge are included in every ticket price you see once you start shopping, so the number on the event page is not a base price waiting for fees to be added. Taxes are not mentioned there; check the order summary for them in your area.

What the all-in price does not tell you is whether the listing is good value. Sellers set resale prices on Vivid Seats, not Vivid Seats itself. Compare the final total for the same section and row on another site, and on the primary seller if anything is left there. ## Vivid Seats Rewards

Vivid Seats Rewards is often a better saving than a code for anyone who buys more than one show a year:

- You get one stamp per ticket bought.
- Ten stamps make one Reward Credit.
- Each stamp is worth the ticket price minus taxes, operating and processing costs, and a Reward Credit is the average value of your ten stamps.
- Stamps and credits expire, on a timeline that depends on your membership level: Rising Star, Super Fan or Icon.

So a credit earned on ten cheap tickets is worth a cheap ticket, not an expensive one. Use it before it expires, and check the credit's value at checkout rather than assuming it covers a whole ticket.

## Staying safe while you look for codes

Coupon searches attract lookalike sites. Enter codes only on vividseats.com or the Vivid Seats app, and never on a page that asks you to sign in to "unlock" a discount. A code that arrives by direct message, or that asks for card details before you have chosen tickets, is not worth testing. ## FAQ

**Do Vivid Seats promo codes work on fees?**

No. Vivid Seats says fixed-amount codes apply to the cost of the tickets only, and service and shipping fees are not affected.

**Why does my Vivid Seats promo code say I have not reached the minimum?**

The minimum is counted on the ticket cost before service and delivery charges. If the tickets alone are under the threshold, the code will not apply, even when the total with fees is above it.

**Does the Vivid Seats price include fees?**

Yes. Vivid Seats uses all-in pricing, and says a service charge and delivery charge are included in every ticket price shown once you start shopping. Check the order summary for any taxes.

**How do I use a Vivid Seats discount from an email?**

If the email has no code, click its banner, choose your tickets in the window that opens, and check out there. Vivid Seats says the discount is then applied automatically.

**How much is a Vivid Seats Reward Credit worth?**

It is the average value of the ten tickets that earned it, after taxes and processing costs are taken off. The credit expires on a timeline set by your membership level.
