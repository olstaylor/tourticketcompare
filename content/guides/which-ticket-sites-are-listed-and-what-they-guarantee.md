**Short answer:** TourTicketCompare links to a small set of ticket sites, and it does not vet their individual sellers or run their guarantees. Each site publishes its own buyer protection. This page lists what each one says, with a link to its own terms, so the promise is read from the source and not from this site. The terms can change, so check the current version before you buy.

## The ticket sites and what they publish

| Ticket site | What it is | What it publishes about protection | Read the terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| SeatGeek | Resale marketplace, and the primary ticketing platform for some leagues and venues | Buyer Guarantee: tickets delivered in time, valid for entry and matching the order. It may provide replacement tickets or a refund, depending on the situation. | [SeatGeek help centre](https://support.seatgeek.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007200174-Can-I-trust-tickets-sold-on-SeatGeek) |
| Vivid Seats | Resale marketplace | Buyer Guarantee: a secure transaction, delivery before the event, and valid, authentic tickets. | [Vivid Seats guarantee](https://www.vividseats.com/guarantee.html) |
| TicketNetwork | Resale marketplace | A 100% Money-Back Guarantee on its company pages. Read the policies page for what it covers, including cancelled and postponed events. | [TicketNetwork policies](https://www.ticketnetwork.com/policies) |
| Ticket Liquidator | Resale marketplace under the same company as TicketNetwork | A 100% Money-Back Guarantee as its company page describes it. | [Ticket Liquidator company page](https://www.ticketliquidator.com/company-info) |

| Ticketmaster | The official primary seller for many shows | Ticketmaster's own terms apply. This is a plain link. | Ticketmaster's site |

None of these guarantees covers a change of plans, buying the wrong date, or paying more than face value. For detail on each, see [Is SeatGeek legit?](/guides/is-seatgeek-legit), [Is Vivid Seats legit?](/guides/is-vivid-seats-legit), [Is TicketNetwork legit?](/guides/is-ticketnetwork-legit), [Is Ticket Liquidator legit?](/guides/is-ticket-liquidator-legit) and [StubHub International explained](/guides/stubhub-international-explained).

## How links are checked

A ticket button appears only when its destination has been confirmed as the page for that artist, date and venue. If it cannot be confirmed, the button is withheld. The site does not sell tickets, handle orders or issue refunds. For a purchase or refund, contact the seller named on your confirmation email.

## How prices are shown

Where a price appears, it comes from the ticket site's own approved partner feed or API and names that site and the time it was captured. It is the lowest listing the site reported for that show at that moment, not a live quote or a checkout total, and fees are often added at checkout. A price that has gone out of date is hidden. Not every site supplies a price for every show. The full method is on the [how it works](/how-it-works) page.

## How the site earns money

Some ticket links earn TourTicketCompare a commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. The links that earn commission appear before the unpaid Ticketmaster link, and that order is not a price ranking. A link has to pass the same checks whether or not it earns commission. Read the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure).

## Spotted a mistake?

If a link goes to the wrong show or a price looks wrong, use the [contact page](/contact) with the page and the ticket site.
