Gracie Abrams tour tickets: listed $34 in Inglewood, $158 in Boston (4.6×)

## Key finding

On Vivid Seats, the lowest listed price for the next Gracie Abrams date in 14 cities in the United States ranged between $34 in Inglewood (Kia Forum, 14 December 2026) and $158 in Boston (TD Garden, 1 March 2027), 4.6 times as much. The median city was listed at $61.

## Method and limits to state when quoting

- One site (Vivid Seats), next date per city, one country (the United States) and one currency (USD); no conversion; no comparison between sites.
- 14 of 14 cities in the United States priced (100%). Unpriced: none.
- Dates in the United Kingdom, Canada, the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, Ireland, Spain are not covered.
- Listed prices at 23 October, 20:40 UTC; prices move. No forecast, no timing advice, no claim about why a city is priced as it is.
- The figures cover the dates TourTicketCompare tracks, not every ticket for the tour and not the concert market.
