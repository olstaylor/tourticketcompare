## Las Vegas leads 2027 dates on 101 tracked major tours, ahead of London and Toronto

Las Vegas has more 2027 dates than any other city across 101 major tours tracked by TourTicketCompare, an independent concert ticket research site. Las Vegas has 72 dates from 18 artists, London 68 and Toronto 61.

Key figures (as of 17 October 2026):

- 2,074 announced 2027 dates across 332 cities and 21 countries.
- The O2 in London has the most dates of any venue: 40.
- The United States hosts 68% of the dates.
- March is the busiest month announced so far.

Full tables by city, venue, country and month: https://tourticketcompare.com/blog/where-the-big-concert-tours-are-playing-in-2027

**Method.** Counts cover announced 2027 shows by the artists TourTicketCompare tracks, each matched to an exact Ticketmaster event and not cancelled or postponed, by the venue's local date. They are not a count of every concert in 2027. Data is rechecked automatically; the newest recheck in this set is 16 October 2026.

**About TourTicketCompare.** TourTicketCompare (tourticketcompare.com) is a free, independent site that shows, for each date on a major tour, which ticket sites list it, with links checked against the exact show. Some links earn a commission. Press resources: https://tourticketcompare.com/press
