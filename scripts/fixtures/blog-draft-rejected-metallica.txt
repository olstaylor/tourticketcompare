## Saturday or a weeknight

The weekday is the clearest practical split. Vancouver, San Antonio, Kansas City, Indianapolis and Salt Lake City fall on a Saturday, which leaves a Sunday to get home if you are travelling.

The rest are weeknights. El Paso, Orchard Park and Cleveland are Tuesdays, Fayetteville is a Wednesday, and Las Vegas and San Diego are Thursdays. If you live close to one of those, a weeknight can be the easier trip. If you are flying in, count the day off work as part of the cost.

## Match the venue's own date

Every date here is the local date at the venue, and the cities span four time zones: Pacific for Las Vegas, San Diego and Vancouver, Mountain for El Paso and Salt Lake City, Central for San Antonio, Fayetteville and Kansas City, and Eastern for Indianapolis, Orchard Park and Cleveland. When a resale listing shows a date, check it against the venue's local date, not your own.

## Las Vegas and Vancouver are listed differently

The [Las Vegas listing](https://www.ticketmaster.com/metallica-life-burns-faster-las-vegas-nevada-01-28-2027/event/170064619015BB05) is at Sphere and carries a different show title from the other cities. Treat it as its own event when comparing, and do not set a Las Vegas listing beside one from a stadium city as if they were the same ticket.

The [Vancouver listing](https://www.ticketmaster.ca/metallica-m72-world-tour-vancouver-british-columbia-05-08-2027/event/1100652ED2CADD90) sits on Ticketmaster's Canadian site. Check which currency a listing shows before comparing it with a date in the US.

## Which resale sites the site has checked

TourTicketCompare's records, verified on 2026-10-15, show checked links at SeatGeek and Vivid Seats for most of these dates. Las Vegas has SeatGeek and TicketNetwork. Fayetteville has SeatGeek only. A checked link means the site found that seller's page for the exact date. It says nothing about seats, quantities or totals, so open each site to see what it actually lists.

## Before you pay

- **The date and city.** Many listings share the artist name and look alike. The date and venue are what separate them, so check both on the listing and on the order summary.
- **The ticket itself.** Section, row, whether seats are together and any restriction notes. The [guide to reading a ticket listing](/guides/how-to-read-a-ticket-listing) covers each label.
- **Who is selling.** Official seller or resale makes a difference to price and protections; the [primary versus resale guide](/guides/primary-vs-resale-concert-tickets) sets it out.
- **When it arrives.** Resale tickets are often transferred close to the show. The [delivery and transfer guide](/guides/ticket-delivery-and-transfer-timing) explains the usual timing.
- **The final total.** Compare checkout totals for the same section and quantity, not the first figure shown.
